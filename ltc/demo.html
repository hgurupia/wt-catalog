How it works
1

Assess

Fifteen sections: referral and homebound status, vitals, home safety, skin and wounds, cognition, function, falls, medications and psychosocial needs.

2

Score

Morse Fall Scale, Braden Scale, PHQ-2 and BMI calculate as you document, with the risk level shown next to each.

3

Generate

The plan of care assembles from the findings, ready for clinician review, physician signature and printing.

Records stay in this browser. Export a record to move it into the clinical record. Generated content is decision support only and needs clinician review and physician signature.
